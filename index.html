Quick Self-Check

How visible is your firm to AI search right now?

Six questions, about a minute. Answer honestly — the gaps are more common than you'd think.

1. Does your site have a dedicated page for each practice area, not just one general page?
2. Do your attorney bios include credentials, case results, and media mentions in detail — not just a photo and a paragraph?
3. Has your firm been quoted or featured in legal, local, or industry press in the last 12 months?
4. Does your site use structured data so search engines and AI systems understand your location, practice areas, and credentials?
5. If you ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for the best lawyer for your practice area in your city, does your firm come up by name?
6. Do you have a standing process for monitoring and updating how your firm appears on AI platforms?
